# Project brief

## My audience and their task

A prospective employer needs to understand what I did, why I chose that approach and what I delivered, quickly. A deeper reviewer should be able to inspect original evidence and try the interactions.

## What I designed this portfolio to do

1. Make my role, project status and outputs visible before a long case study.
2. Offer both an exploratory project board and a quick-scan recruiter route.
3. Put original work beside concise explanations of the problem, my actions and the result.
4. Make portfolio prototypes usable while identifying which interactions were built for this site.
5. Show my approach to AI-assisted design through a documented, working example.

## Review criteria

- Every case identifies the role and delivery status; proposed targets are not presented as measured results.
- Important actions work with a keyboard, have visible focus and remain understandable with motion reduced.
- Mobile layouts retain legible content and working navigation.
- AI summaries use only the fixed fictional histories, expose no key and have a labelled prewritten alternative.
- A fresh clone can run without credentials and pass the documented checks.

These are acceptance criteria for this implementation, not claims of measured hiring conversion or a completed accessibility certification.

## Scope

Selected Royal London, Braze, Express Capital, Fuse and Nevis work, plus an independent Hilo concept, career summary and CV download. No real medical records, notifications, emails, financial applications or external account actions are performed by the demos.
