## My independent XPS exploration

I designed and built a connected retirement-request concept with member and administrator perspectives. A fictional sample moves through review and correction states, with a shared history and an explicit next owner. My output is a working prototype; improved understanding and fewer status enquiries remain hypotheses. I use the public XPS brand direction without claiming commission, endorsement or access to its private platforms. [My brief, reference and research plan](xps/brief.md) record the evidence and limits.

## Fidelity rules

- Royal London and Braze navigation replay supplied original screens; missing source screens must not be invented as historic work.
- Braze ROI inputs can be edited, but unavailable proprietary calculations must remain unavailable. Export examples are original sample designs, not generated results for edited inputs.
- Additional portfolio interactions are labelled as new work. Do not turn an idea, visualisation or target into a shipped outcome.
- Every health scenario and exported GP brief is fictional. Heart rate alone is not treated as proof of inactivity.

The source portfolio PDF and CV are deliberately excluded from this public repo and its Git history. Source and CV links point to the live website, where the owner manages those downloads. The complete Keynote, presenter notes and private correspondence are excluded. [Asset provenance](assets.md) maps the retained visuals to the supplied sources; republication rights and any internal details should be reviewed before adding more client material.
