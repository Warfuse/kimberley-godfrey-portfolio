ROYAL LONDON / EMPLOYER WEBSITE / LAUNCHED 2024

I redesigned the
employer website.

The problem & task

Employers had to work through PDFs and organisation-led menus to do routine pension administration. I redesigned the site’s information architecture, navigation and page templates.

What I did & why

I created a “Manage your scheme” hub, task-led menus and web guides. Administration gained a clear home; content became readable, searchable and linkable.

What I delivered

I delivered the redesign for the summer 2024 launch. I converted 42 PDFs into web content and replaced seven flat links with five grouped menus.
