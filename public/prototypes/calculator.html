Show the maths & assumptions

Purchase loan = purchase × purchase LTV. Renovation loan = renovation × rehab LTV. Interest = total loan × annual rate × months ÷ 12. Full renovation loan is assumed drawn from day one; no compounding.

Origination points apply to the whole loan. Mortgage tax applies to the loan; title and transfer tax apply to purchase. Broker fee applies to resale. Holding costs = monthly property costs × months. Other costs are a fixed amount.

Project cost = purchase + renovation + all modelled fees and holding costs. Profit = resale − project cost. Cash required = project cost − loan. Cash-on-cash ROI = profit ÷ cash required. This simplified cash figure includes selling costs and is not a draw schedule.

Demo thresholds: Losing = loss; Weak = nonnegative profit and ROI below 10%; Good = 10–19.99%; Strong = 20%+. These are portfolio-demo choices, not lender-approved thresholds. “Unrated” means zero cash denominator.

Excludes income/capital-gains tax, draw timing, extension fees, prepayment penalties and unentered costs. This model is not financial advice.
